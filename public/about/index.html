Same room. Same game.

A friend opens a laptop at the kitchen table. You pull up a chair and take out your phone. We want you to be able to join the game right there, without going home to a second PC.

We’re working on local multiplayer so you can play together even when there’s no internet. We’re exploring ways for a phone and a PC to connect over the same Wi-Fi network or directly over Bluetooth.

Why we keep the art simple

You shouldn’t need a new phone to play with friends. We use pixel art and simple 3D models to help our games run smoothly on older devices.
